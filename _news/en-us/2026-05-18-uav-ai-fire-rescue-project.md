A ministry-level research project led by Dr. Pham Hoang Anh has been listed by PTIT Faculty of Electronics Engineering 1 in its [scientific projects page](https://sea.ptit.edu.vn/nghien-cuu/cac-du-an-khoa-hoc/).

- Project code: **ĐT.37-01/26**
- Principal investigator: **Dr. Pham Hoang Anh**
- Project title: **Research and development of a UAV and AI system to support firefighting, rescue, and emergency response in Bac Ninh province**

The project targets a UAV platform equipped with optical and thermal cameras, a ground station, and system software for UAV monitoring and control on digital maps, autonomous route planning, camera-data collection and visualization, and AI-based fire-scene analysis and human detection. The project also includes building datasets for fire-scene analysis and human detection.
